TransitOS is a free public transport app for real-time metro and tram travel. Check live schedules and arrivals, plan a journey with transfers, and follow the whole network map: everything you need to move around Valencia with Metrovalencia, without ads and without tracking.

LIVE ARRIVALS AND SCHEDULES

Save your favourite metro and tram stops and see the next arrivals at a glance. Each departure shows the line, the destination and how many minutes are left, updated live. Perfect for the daily commute and for checking the last train of the day.

JOURNEY PLANNER WITH TRANSFERS

Plan any trip across the metro and tram network. Search an origin and a destination and TransitOS finds the best routes, with every transfer, the lines to take, the walking time and the full journey duration. Choose to leave now or to arrive by a specific time, and set a minimum transfer buffer so you only get connections you can comfortably make.

METROVALENCIA NETWORK MAP

Explore the full Metrovalencia network map, with all lines in their official colours and every station and connection. Zoom in, check where you need to change lines, and download the official PDF map for offline use.

SERVICE ALERTS

Disruptions, delays and planned works are shown before you leave, so you can pick an alternative route instead of finding out at the station.

FAVOURITES AND SAVED ROUTES

Star the stops you use every day and save your regular journeys, from home to work or to the airport, with a custom label for one-tap access.

DARK MODE AND AMOLED

System, light, dark and AMOLED pure-black themes, easy on the eyes at night and lighter on your battery on OLED screens.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

TransitOS is private by default. No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no account. Your location data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The app talks only to the public transport operator's public API over HTTPS, straight from your phone.

MULTILINGUAL

Available in English, Spanish and Valencian, with the language you choose applied instantly.

OPEN SOURCE

TransitOS is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. The full source code is at github.com/IgnacioLD/TransitOS, and contributions, bug reports and translations are welcome.

Built first for Metrovalencia (FGV), the metro and tram network of Valencia, and designed to grow into a multi-operator public transport app: more cities and operators are on the way.

TransitOS is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Metrovalencia or FGV.
